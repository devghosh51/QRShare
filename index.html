QR-Share

Send text and small files between phones with just a screen and a camera.

Fast speeds need good light, a bright screen and a steady hand. If the receiver misses frames, use Resend missing frames after the first pass.

Nothing leaves your device. Files up to 10 MB are supported, but big files take a long time (see the estimate after you tap Make).

Hold the other phone steady until its ring is full and green.
